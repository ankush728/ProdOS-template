## Introduction (2 minutes)

"Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. I'm [name] from PSTrax, and we're conducting research to better understand [topic area] in fire departments.

This conversation will take about [X] minutes. There are no right or wrong answers - we're genuinely interested in learning about your experience and perspective.

Everything you share will be kept confidential. May I record this conversation for note-taking purposes?"

**[START RECORDING]**

---

## Warm-Up (3-5 minutes)

1. Can you tell me a bit about your role and your department?
   - How long have you been in this position?
   - How many [units/personnel/stations] do you oversee?

2. What does a typical day or week look like for you?

---

## Current Process (10-15 minutes)

### Understanding Current State

3. Walk me through how you [do X task] today, from start to finish.
   - [Follow-up: What happens first?]
   - [Follow-up: And then what?]

4. What tools or systems do you currently use for this?

5. How often do you [perform this task]?

6. Who else is involved in this process?
   - [Follow-up: What role do they play?]

7. What happens after [this step]?
   - [Follow-up: How does this connect to other workflows?]

---

## Pain Points & Challenges (10-15 minutes)

### Identifying Frustrations

8. What frustrates you most about [this process]?
   - [Follow-up: Can you give me a specific example?]

9. What errors or issues occur most frequently?
   - [Follow-up: What causes these errors?]

10. Have you developed any workarounds to deal with these problems?
    - [Follow-up: How well do those workarounds work?]

11. What would happen if you stopped doing [this task]? What are the consequences?

12. On a scale of 1-10, how critical is solving this problem? Why that number?

---

## [MOAT DIMENSION SECTIONS - CUSTOMIZE BASED ON TOPIC]

### 🎯 Proprietary Data Opportunities

13. [DATA] How do you currently track [X] over time?
    - [Follow-up: What patterns have you noticed?]

14. [DATA] If you could see trends across all your [units/stations/department], what insights would be most valuable to you?

15. [DATA] What data do you collect now that you wish you could analyze better?

**PM Note:** [Explain what proprietary data opportunity this probes for and why it's defensible - e.g., "Probing for longitudinal data that competitors can't replicate without years of operation"]

---

### 🧠 Tacit Knowledge Capture

16. [TACIT] When an experienced [role] examines [equipment/situation], what do they notice that a rookie would miss?
    - [Follow-up: How did you learn to notice these things?]

17. [TACIT] What "gut feelings" or intuition guide your decisions about [X]?
    - [Follow-up: Can you give me an example?]

18. [TACIT] What unwritten rules exist for [this task] that aren't in any manual or training?

**PM Note:** [Explain what tacit knowledge opportunity this reveals - e.g., "Expert judgment that could be codified before NFPA standardizes it"]

---

### 🔗 Workflow Embeddedness

19. [WORKFLOW] What other systems or tools do you use during [this task]?
    - [Follow-up: How do they connect?]

20. [WORKFLOW] How does [this data/information] flow to other departments or systems?

21. [WORKFLOW] What downstream decisions or actions depend on [this information]?

**PM Note:** [Explain integration opportunities and switching cost implications - e.g., "Integration points that would make PSTrax central hub"]

---

### 🤝 Human-in-the-Loop & Trust

22. [HITL] What parts of [this task] could NEVER be automated? Why?
    - [Follow-up: What makes human judgment essential here?]

23. [TRUST] What would make you trust an AI recommendation about [X]?
    - [Follow-up: What would need to be proven?]

24. [HITL] Who needs to verify or approve [this outcome] before it's considered official?

**PM Note:** [Explain HITL design opportunity and trust-building requirements - e.g., "Human validation creates training data AND builds trust for future automation"]

---

### ⚖️ Mission-Critical Stakes

25. [STAKES] What happens if [this task] is missed or done incorrectly?
    - [Follow-up: Has this ever happened? What were the consequences?]

26. [STAKES] Has your department ever faced scrutiny (audit, investigation, lawsuit) related to [this area]?
    - [Follow-up: What documentation did you have to provide?]

27. [STAKES] What documentation is required to prove [compliance/readiness] during an investigation or audit?

**PM Note:** [Explain how mission-critical stakes create defensibility opportunity - e.g., "Reinforces that this is about surviving litigation, not just saving time"]

---

## Wrap-Up (5 minutes)

28. If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing about [this process], what would it be?

29. Is there anything else about [topic] that I should have asked about but didn't?

30. Do you know anyone else who might have valuable perspective on this topic that I should talk to?
    - [If yes: Can you make an introduction?]

---

## Closing

"Thank you so much for your time and insights today. This has been incredibly valuable for our research.

I'll send you a summary of our conversation for your review within the next week. If you think of anything else after we hang up, please don't hesitate to reach out.

Do you have any questions for me?"

**[STOP RECORDING]**
